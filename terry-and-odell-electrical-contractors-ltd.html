Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our electrical services, pricing and what to expect when you work with us.

Still have questions?

Call us: 07554 017 456

Are you a registered electrical contractor?

Yes, Terry & Odell Electrical Contractors Ltd is a fully registered company. All our electricians are fully qualified and we carry comprehensive insurance. We can provide all necessary certificates and documentation for any work completed.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, we provide free, no-obligation quotes for all electrical work. We'll visit your property, assess the job, and provide a detailed written quote. The price we quote is the price you pay - no hidden costs or surprises.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Gillingham, Dorset and cover the surrounding areas including Shaftesbury, Mere, Wincanton, Sturminster Newton, Yeovil, and throughout North Dorset and South Somerset. Contact us to check if we cover your area.

Do you issue electrical certificates?

Yes, we issue all appropriate electrical certificates including Electrical Installation Certificates, Minor Works Certificates, and EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Reports). These are essential for insurance, selling your property, or landlord compliance.

How long does a typical rewire take?

A full rewire typically takes between 5-10 days depending on the size of the property and the complexity of the work. We'll give you an accurate timeline during our initial quote visit and keep you informed throughout the project.
