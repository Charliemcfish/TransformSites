Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our plumbing and heating services across Sherborne and the wider Dorset area.

Still have questions?

Call us: 07843 868726

Are you Gas Safe and OFTEC registered?

Yes, we are both Gas Safe and OFTEC registered. This dual certification means we're qualified to work on both gas and oil-fired heating systems, which is particularly important for rural properties in Dorset. You can verify our registration numbers on the official websites.

Do you work on period and listed properties?

Absolutely. We have extensive experience working with period properties including Grade II listed buildings and thatched cottages. We understand the unique challenges these properties present and take care to sympathetically integrate modern heating technology without compromising the building's character.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Sherborne and cover the wider Dorset area including Yeovil, Dorchester, Sturminster Newton, Blandford Forum and surrounding villages. Please get in touch to confirm we can reach your location.

Do you provide landlord gas safety certificates?

Yes, we provide Gas Safety certificates (CP12) for landlords, which are a legal requirement for rental properties. We can arrange annual checks to ensure your properties remain compliant and your tenants safe.

Can you provide a written quote before starting work?

Yes, we always provide free, no-obligation written quotes before any work begins. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden costs. The price we quote is the price you pay - unless you request additional work during the project.
