Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, pricing and what to expect when you work with us.

Still have questions?

Call us: 01234 567 890

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No, we don't charge call-out fees for standard appointments within our service area. For emergency call-outs outside normal hours, a small additional fee may apply, but we'll always tell you upfront before attending.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes, all our engineers are fully Gas Safe registered and undergo regular training to stay up-to-date with the latest regulations and techniques. You can verify our registration number on the official Gas Safe website.

How quickly can you respond to emergencies?

We aim to respond to emergency calls within 60 minutes. Our 24/7 emergency line ensures you can reach us anytime, day or night. We understand that plumbing emergencies can't wait, so we prioritise these calls.

Do you offer guarantees on your work?

Absolutely. We provide a 12-month guarantee on all labour and workmanship. Any parts or appliances we install come with their own manufacturer warranties, which we'll explain clearly at the time of installation.

Can you provide a written quote before starting work?

Yes, we always provide free, no-obligation written quotes before any work begins. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden costs. The price we quote is the price you pay – unless you request additional work.
